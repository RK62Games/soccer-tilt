How to play

  • Tilt your phone to roll the ball, just like turning the knobs of a wooden labyrinth.
  • Follow the white line from START into the goal.
  • Do not let the ball drop into a hole. The holes are numbered along the line, so you can see how far you got.
  • Hold the phone in any comfortable position. Before every level the game takes that position as "level".
  • Score fast for three stars. Sliding along the wooden walls is allowed.

Questions

The ball rolls even when I hold the phone still

You have probably changed how you hold the phone since the level started. Tap the bubble level at the bottom left of the screen and the current position becomes "level" again. Pausing and resuming the game does the same.

The ball is too fast or too slow

Tap the pause button and choose the tilt sensitivity: Low, Normal or High.

Can I play lying down or in a car?

Lying down works: tap the bubble level to recenter whenever you change position. In a moving car, bus or train the motion of the vehicle also moves the ball.

What does "Continue from here" do?

When the ball drops, you can watch a short video and the ball goes back on the line just before the hole. Your time keeps running. Watching the video is always optional.

How do I get three stars?

Score faster than the time shown at the bottom of the screen. The goal screen tells you the times for two and three stars.

Why does the game show ads?

Ads keep the game free. An ad may appear between levels.

How do I change my privacy choices?

In the EU, UK and Switzerland, open the game, tap the (i) button on the title screen and choose Privacy choices. You can turn off app tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Read more in the privacy policy.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. You can start over with Reset progress under the (i) button.
