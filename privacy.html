Tiltwood

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Who we are

Tiltwood is an iPhone game made by Riku Kruuse, an independent developer in Finland ("I", "me"). If you have questions about this policy or your data, email rk62games@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and I do not collect your name, email or other personal information.
  • The game reads your phone's motion sensors only to roll the ball. These readings are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device and usage data to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.
  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time.

Motion sensors

Tiltwood is played by tilting the phone. While a level is on the screen, the game reads which way is down from the phone's accelerometer and gyroscope (through Apple's Core Motion). The readings are used only to move the ball on the screen. They are not saved and they never leave your device.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your unlocked levels, stars, best times, how far you got on each level and your settings (sound, vibration and tilt sensitivity) on your iPhone. This data is not sent to me or to anyone else. It is deleted when you delete the app, or when you choose Reset progress in the game.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC. When ads are shown, Google may collect and process:

  • device information, such as model, operating system version and language,
  • the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in the iOS question,
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and about app performance and crashes.

Google uses this data to show ads, measure how they perform, prevent fraud and abuse, and, if you have agreed, to show ads that match your interests. Google acts as an independent controller for this data. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Watching a video to continue a level after the ball drops is always optional.

Your choices

Consent in the EU, UK and Switzerland

When you start the game for the first time in these areas, Google's consent form asks whether you allow personalised ads. To change your choice later, tap the (i) button on the title screen and choose Privacy choices. If you do not give consent, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads.

App tracking on iOS

iOS asks whether the game may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Your rights

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to object to its processing and to withdraw consent. Because I do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best handled through Google's tools; I am happy to help by email. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. In Finland this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

Children

The game is suitable for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

These web pages

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, when you visit them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages themselves use no cookies and load nothing from other sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
